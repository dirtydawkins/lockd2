LOCKD ACCOUNT

Privacy
Notice.

This notice describes the account data that the current LOCKD website is designed to store. It is a working product notice and has not yet had qualified Kenyan legal review.

Information in your LOCKD account

Supabase Auth manages your login credentials and session. LOCKD does not store plaintext passwords in its marketplace tables. Your account profile can store a display name, profile image URL supplied by a sign-in provider, preferred currency, language, and a discovery area that you choose to enter.

Saved listings are linked to your Supabase account and include a small private snapshot of the listing details as they were when saved. You can remove saved listings from your account.

Bookings and payments

Online reservations, checkout, payment collection, refunds and payouts are not enabled in the current LOCKD website. The account page does not create or display a fabricated booking history. Existing listing date checks do not reserve inventory.

Account security

Your account data is protected with database policies that are intended to restrict profile, saved-listing and deletion-request records to the signed-in account. Google or Apple sign-in works only if its provider credentials and redirect settings are enabled in Supabase. Supabase email delivery configuration also affects verification and password reset messages.

Access or deletion requests

From your account’s Login & security section, you can submit an account deletion request. A request is stored for review; submission does not immediately delete your account. An operator must review active listings, any operational obligations and records that may need to be retained before completing a deletion. LOCKD does not currently provide automated self-service deletion.

Location and preferences

LOCKD does not automatically save precise device location to your profile. If you enter a discovery area in Preferences, that text is saved with your profile and can be cleared there.

Retention and contact

Retention periods, data access fulfillment and contact details for privacy requests require an operator policy and legal review. Until an official support contact is configured, use the deletion request in your LOCKD account. Do not submit identity documents, passwords or payment secrets through listing forms.

Last updated 28 October 2026. This notice is not a statement of legal compliance. LOCKD requires qualified legal review for the Kenyan operating model before transactional launch.
