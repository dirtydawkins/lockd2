LOCKD ACCOUNT

Privacy
Notice.

This notice describes the account data that the current LOCKD website is designed to store. It is a working product notice and has not yet had qualified Kenyan legal review.

Information in your LOCKD account

Supabase Auth manages your login credentials and session. LOCKD does not store plaintext passwords in its marketplace tables. Your account profile can store a display name, profile photo you upload or one supplied by Google, preferred currency, language, and a discovery area that you choose to enter.

Uploaded profile photos are stored privately, cropped to a square and can be replaced or removed in Personal information. Signed photo links expire after one hour. Saved listings are linked to your Supabase account and include a small private snapshot of the listing details as they were when saved. You can remove saved listings from your account.

Bookings and payments

Online reservations, checkout, payment collection, refunds and payouts are not enabled in the current LOCKD website. The account page does not create or display a fabricated booking history. Existing listing date checks do not reserve inventory.

Account security

Your account data is protected with database policies that are intended to restrict profile, saved-listing and deletion-request records to the signed-in account. Google processes sign-in when you choose it. Account verification and password recovery use the email service configured for LOCKD.

Access or deletion requests

From your account’s Login & security section, you can submit an account deletion request. A request is stored for review; submission does not immediately delete your account. An operator must review active listings, any operational obligations and records that may need to be retained before completing a deletion. LOCKD does not currently provide automated self-service deletion.

Location and preferences

You can choose an area or share your current location to find nearby rentals. Device coordinates are rounded before being sent to LOCKD and are not saved to your profile. We may use an approximate area supplied by our hosting provider; we do not retain your raw IP for discovery. A place you choose is remembered for up to 90 days in a necessary preference cookie containing its locality reference and name, never device coordinates. Clear location removes this choice and recent places. Town and neighbourhood searches are sent to Geoapify. Map tiles are served by OpenFreeMap, which receives ordinary network requests and the area shown on the map. Lister addresses and pickup instructions are kept separately from public listing details. If you save a discovery area in account Preferences, that text is saved with your profile and can be cleared there.

Retention and contact

Retention periods, data access fulfillment and contact details for privacy requests require an operator policy and legal review. Until an official support contact is configured, use the deletion request in your LOCKD account. Do not submit identity documents, passwords or payment secrets through listing forms.

Private address evidence

You may choose utility, tenancy, institutional, family or other supporting evidence. LOCKD stores your declared address, consent, corrections and review history privately for authorized verification work. Reading a document does not authenticate its issuer.

Document intake remains disabled until malware scanning, a retention policy, processor arrangements and legal review are approved. The proposed supporting-document retention is 30 days, with earlier withdrawal available. A withdrawal starts a private deletion workflow and can revoke an address approval based on that evidence. It does not erase operational records that require a separately approved retention policy. No raw documents are sent to unrelated AI providers.

Necessary browser storage

LOCKD uses necessary browser storage for the signed-in session, security and preferences you choose to save. Private message access is sealed in browser storage and associated with the existing LOCKD account. If you accept optional cookies, LOCKD remembers anonymous visit and session identifiers and basic page and search counts in first-party cookies. No search text, precise device coordinates, private account details or message contents are included. Rejecting optional cookies removes these usage cookies while keeping sign-in and chosen-area preferences working. No third-party advertising or analytics scripts are currently enabled.

Private messages and reports

LOCKD stores encrypted messages and the information needed to deliver them. Private message access is prepared automatically through your signed-in account and kept on your browser. New contact codes are remembered; a changed code must be checked before sending. Protect your account and approved browsers. Losing every approved browser can make old messages unreadable.

Messages are available for 90 days, with expired message content removed by scheduled maintenance. Conversation references and safety reports may be retained for security and resolving concerns. Administrators cannot read ordinary messages. A concern report shares its category and selected message references. Message text is shared only when the report preview explicitly offers that option and you agree. Attachments are not currently available.

Use your account’s deletion request for privacy enquiries. Browser security remains important, and no independent security certification or guarantee is claimed.

Last updated 2 November 2026. This notice is not a statement of legal compliance. LOCKD requires qualified legal review for the Kenyan operating model before transactional launch.
