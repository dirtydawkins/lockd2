How privacy works

Messages are protected on your device. LOCKD can review only the messages you choose to include in a report. Contact codes are remembered automatically; unexpected changes are flagged before sending.

Keep your browser and LOCKD account secure. Removing access disconnects all linked devices. Someone with your unlocked message access could read past messages. Losing every approved browser can make old messages unreadable.
