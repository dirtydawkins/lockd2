Open the approval file on your new browser. Keep these files private and finish within 10 minutes.

Remove message access

This removes the account’s current message key. You may lose access to old messages unless another approved browser still has the original access.

How privacy works

Messages are protected on your device. LOCKD can review only the messages you choose to include in a report. Contact codes are remembered automatically; unexpected changes are flagged before sending.

Keep your browser and LOCKD account secure. Approved browsers currently share one message identity and cannot be removed separately. Someone with your unlocked message access could read past messages. Losing every approved browser can make old messages unreadable.
